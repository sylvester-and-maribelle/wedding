我们的故事

我们的故事，是从一辆二手车开始的。

在加拿大 Kelowna 读完第一个学期后，Maribelle 终于存够了钱， 想买一辆二手车。不过，她对车真的完全不懂。 刚好她认识 Sylvester——两人有共同的朋友，又同样来自马来西亚， 所以就找他帮忙给点意见，看看应该买什么车比较好。

本来只是问问车的事情，结果 Sylvester 好像有点热心过头了。😂 两个人就这样越聊越多，慢慢熟了起来。 没想到才过了一个月，就这样在一起了。

八年后，我们还在一起，那辆车竟然也还在！ 这些年来，它陪着我们在加拿大跑了超过 4,000 公里， 从 Kelowna 到 Winnipeg，再一路来到 Toronto。 最好笑的是，现在这辆车反而是 Sylvester 在开， 而且大多数时候都是拿来开去机场上班。

所以这样算起来，Maribelle 当年买了一辆二手车， 最后还附送一个老公。好像还蛮值得的。😂

OUR STORY

It all started with a second-hand car.

After one semester in Kelowna, Canada, Maribelle finally saved enough money to buy one — but knew absolutely nothing about cars. So she reached out to Sylvester, a mutual friend, fellow Malaysian, and apparently the most qualified used-car expert she knew.

She asked for car advice. He gave her more attention than expected. One thing led to another, and within a month, they were officially together.

Eight years later, the relationship is still going strong — and so is the car. It has travelled more than 4,000 km across Canada, from Kelowna to Winnipeg and now Toronto. Ironically, Sylvester is the one driving it these days, mostly to the airport for work.

So technically, Maribelle bought a car and somehow got a husband too. Not a bad deal.
