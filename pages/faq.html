常见问题

中文版

最迟什么时候要回复 RSVP？

请在 2027年5月20日之前 回复 RSVP， 方便我们确认出席人数和安排座位。:)

我可以带多一位宾客吗？

请参考您的邀请，看是否有包括同行宾客。 如果不确定，也可以直接问我们。

可以带小朋友出席吗？

我们当然很欢迎大家的小朋友！不过因为场地人数有限， 目前只能安排邀请中有注明的小朋友出席。 请参考您的邀请确认出席名单，谢谢大家的体谅！

婚宴当天要在哪里停车？

可以把车停在 Oceanus Mall 6楼， 不需要拿停车票。 不过如果方便的话，我们也很鼓励大家搭 Grab， 这样当天不用烦停车，喝了酒也可以轻轻松松回家。

当天要穿什么？

婚宴着装为 正式 / 鸡尾酒会着装。 颜色没有限制，穿自己喜欢的颜色就好！ 只希望大家避免短裤、拖鞋或太休闲的服装。

提交 RSVP 后还可以更改吗？

可以。如果提交 RSVP 后行程有变， 请尽早告诉我们，我们可以帮您更新出席人数和座位安排。

教堂婚姻祝福礼和婚宴都一定要参加吗？

不一定哦！如果时间上没办法两场都参加，我们完全明白。 RSVP 时只需要选择您可以出席的场次就好。 不管在哪一场见到您，我们都会很开心。💛

如果还有问题，可以找谁？

有任何问题都可以直接在 WhatsApp 或 Instagram 找我们两位，我们很乐意帮忙！

English

What’s the RSVP deadline?

Please RSVP by May 20, 2027 so we can get an accurate headcount. :)

Can I bring a guest?

Please refer to your invitation to see whether a plus-one is included. If you’re unsure, feel free to check with us.

Are kids welcome?

We love your little ones! Due to venue capacity, however, we can only accommodate children who are specifically included on the invitation. Please refer to your invitation to confirm the guests in your party. Thank you for understanding!

Where can I park?

Parking is available at Oceanus Mall, Level 6, and no parking ticket is required. However, we encourage guests to take Grab where possible so you can arrive and head home comfortably without worrying about parking.

What’s the dress code?

Formal / Cocktail Attire is kindly requested. Feel free to wear any colour you love. We simply ask guests to avoid shorts, slippers, and overly casual attire.

Can I change my RSVP after submitting it?

Yes. If your plans change after submitting your RSVP, please let us know as soon as possible so we can update the seating arrangements.

Do I need to attend both the Church Marriage Blessing and the Wedding Dinner?

Not at all! We completely understand if you’re unable to join us for both. Please RSVP for whichever celebration(s) you’ll be able to attend — we’ll be happy to celebrate with you whenever you can make it. 💛

Who can I reach out to if I have questions?

Feel free to reach out to either of us on WhatsApp or Instagram. We’re happy to help!
